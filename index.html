What We Do

All About Us

Glorix is a creative collective redefining the underground music and event scene. We bridge artists, audiences, and brands through immersive cultural experiences. Fusing music, fashion, and art, Glorix embodies raw expression and originality. At its heart, it’s a movement dedicated to empowering and elevating independent talent.

Our Past Collaborations

Glorix has collaborated with a range of venues, brands, and creative collectives across Delhi-NCR. Each partnership reflects our dedication to crafting authentic underground experiences that merge music, art, and culture into one unified vibe.

Our Expertise

Glorix specializes in curating high-energy music events that bring the culture to life. From concept to execution, every detail — sound, stage, visuals, and vibe — is crafted to create unforgettable experiences. Our team blends creativity with precision, turning every show into a celebration of not only music and art, but for the community too.
